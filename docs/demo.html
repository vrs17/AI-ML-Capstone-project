2Choose a car photo

How to read this. The system answers only when it is confident the car is one of the five known models. A car that is none of them is returned as others (rejected), and a photo the model is unsure about is abstained and would be routed to a human. That is the design: precision on the answers that matter, not a guess on every photo.
