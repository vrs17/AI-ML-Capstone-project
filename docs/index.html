01 · Context

The project in one clear story.

Replace the placeholders with facts from your README, results, screenshots, or working demo. Do not invent business impact.

The problem

No public tool — or dataset — recognizes the specific cars common on Uzbekistan’s roads. Three of the five targets are near-identical sedans (Cobalt, Gentra, Nexia 3), and a real camera constantly sees cars that are not on the list at all, which a normal classifier is forced to mislabel.

Who uses it

Owners and operators of gas stations and car washes who want automatic counts of which car models visit their site.

Automatic model counts from camera photosWrong answers must be rare, not just frequent-and-fast
02 · System

How it works, without the notebook noise.

Keep this to three or four stages. A visitor should understand the route in under twenty seconds.

Photo in

A single car photo. The dataset was collected from public avtoelon.uz listings, deduplicated, and human-verified.

Detect & crop

A pretrained YOLO detector locates the car and crops to it, dropping photos that contain no car (interiors, engines, documents).

Classify

A fine-tuned ConvNeXt-Tiny with a sub-center ArcFace head at 384px separates the five look-alike models plus an others class.

Answer or abstain

Temperature-scaled confidence decides: name the model, reject it as others, or abstain and route the photo to a human.

What this proves

The evaluation is leakage-freeThe split groups photos by listing, not by photo, so no car appears on both sides. Zero cross-split listings, asserted at build time.
It generalizes — it did not memorizeSealed test 0.950 versus validation 0.949. A memorizing model would drop sharply on the unseen set.
The look-alike sedans are separatedAfter the ArcFace margin head, Cobalt / Gentra / Nexia 3 cross-confusions fell to single digits; all three reach F1 ≥ 0.953.

Known limitation

The others class — every car outside the five — is the weakest at 0.789 F1, because it is not one thing but many. All training photos also come from a single marketplace, so posed listing images may not match real street or camera conditions; a separate street-photo test set is planned. The 99%-precision abstention threshold is implemented but its operating point has not yet been measured on the sealed test.
