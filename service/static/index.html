What this screen is for

A diagnostic view of one frame — the same two stages the live console runs per tracked vehicle, with the intermediate numbers exposed. The console is what an operator watches; this is what you open when you want to know why.

If a car is recognised confidently here but not on camera, the usual cause is size: this crop is taken at full photo resolution, while a camera may see the same car only a hundred pixels wide.
