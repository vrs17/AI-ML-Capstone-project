How it decides

Two stages. A detector finds every vehicle and crops it; a fine-grained classifier names the model from that crop. In video each car keeps a track across frames, is looked at several times, and every look is judged against the calibrated threshold — the track takes the majority of the looks that passed, so one blurred frame cannot decide anything.

It answers only when it can hold that precision. Anything else is rejected as outside the catalogue or passed to staff — never guessed.
